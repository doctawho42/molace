====================================================================================================
THE IDENTITY, STATED AND NOT TESTED
====================================================================================================
  R2 = r^2 - (r - b)^2 - d^2, with b = sd(pred)/sd(y) and d = (mean y - mean pred)/sd(y)
  max residual over the 379 non-constant cells: 1.887e-15
  calibration penalty (r-b)^2 + d^2: min 3.369e-05, median 0.0139, max 0.2476, negative on 0 cells
  So r2 = R2 + penalty with penalty >= 0: the squared Pearson correlation is the
  coefficient of determination plus a non-negative calibration term, and being invariant to
  affine rescaling of the prediction it cannot see that term. This is algebra, true before
  the data were read, and it is NOT offered as a measurement.

  per-arm mean penalty: MR 0.0153  1-NN 0.0921  kNN 0.0192  RFR 0.0145  SVR 0.0035
  A neighbour rule has b near 1 by construction and a regularised kernel machine shrinks b
  toward r, so a penalty gap between those arms follows from their construction and is NOT
  evidence about their modelling choices.
